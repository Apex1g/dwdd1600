Digital Citizenship

What is Digital Citezenship?

Digital citizenship means using technology and the internet in a safe, responsible, respectful, and ethical way.

What is Digital Law?

Digital law refers to the rules and laws that govern how people use technology, computers, the internet, and digital information.

What is AUP?

It is a set of rules for how people are allowed to use computers, the internet, and other technology at a school, workplace, or organization.

What is Plagiarism?

Plagiarism is when you use someone else's words, ideas, or work and claim them as your own without giving credit.

In simple terms: Plagiarism means taking someone else's work without giving them credit.

What is Academic Dishonesty?

Academic dishonesty means cheating or being dishonest in schoolwork or academic activities.

In simple terms: Academic dishonesty is breaking school rules to gain an unfair advantage.

What are the rules for AI Generated Content?

In simple terms: Use AI honestly, responsibly, and according to your school's rules.
