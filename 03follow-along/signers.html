Who Were the Signers

Famous Signers

Some of the most famous signers of the Declaration of Independance include:

John Hancock
He was president of the Continental Congress and is famous for his large, bold signature on the Declaration of Independence. His name is often associated with the phrase "put your John Hancock here," meaning to sign your name.
Thomas Jefferson
He was the principal author of the Declaration of Independence and later became the third president of the United States.
Benjamin Franklin
A famous inventor, scientist, writer, and diplomat, Franklin helped gain French support for the American Revolution.
John Adams
Adams strongly advocated independence and helped persuade other delegates to support breaking away from Great Britain. He later became the second U.S. president.
Samuel Adams
He was a leading opponent of British taxes and policies and helped organize resistance to British rule, including the movement that led to the Boston Tea Party.
Roger Sherman
Sherman was one of the few people who signed the Continental Association, the Declaration of Independence, the Articles of Confederation, and the U.S. Constitution.
Benjamin Rush
A physician and educator from Pennsylvania, Rush supported independence and later helped establish important American medical and educational institutions.
Richard Henry Lee
He introduced the resolution calling for American independence.
Charles Carroll of Carrollton
He was the last surviving signer of the Declaration of Independence, dying in 1832.
Caesar Rodney
He helped secure Delaware's vote for independence.
George Walton
He was a Georgia delegate who signed the Declaration of Independence.

Each of these individuals played a significant role int he founding of the United States and is remembered for their courage in signing the Declaration, an act considered treasonous by the british Crown.
